Puckbar Privacy Policy

Effective date: [Month Day, 2026]

Puckbar is a macOS menu bar app developed by Battersea House (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the app does and doesn’t handle.

Information we collect

None. Puckbar doesn’t require an account and doesn’t collect, store, or transmit any personal information to us. There are no analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs in the app.

Information stored on your Mac

Your settings, such as your chosen team, are stored locally on your Mac and are never sent to us. Deleting the app removes them.

Network requests

To work, Puckbar connects to the internet to:

  • Fetch schedules and scores from [schedule data provider name].
  • Download app configuration from a server we operate (battersea.dev).

Like any web request, these services may receive standard technical information such as your IP address and the time of the request. We don’t log or use this information to identify you. The data provider’s handling of requests is governed by its own privacy policy.

Ticket links

Puckbar may show links to buy tickets for upcoming games. Clicking one opens a third-party website in your browser, and that site’s own privacy policy and cookies apply. Puckbar doesn’t share any information with these sites.

Children

Puckbar doesn’t knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the effective date above.
