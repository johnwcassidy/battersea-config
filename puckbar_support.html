Puckbar Support

Puckbar keeps your team’s recent results, upcoming games, and live scores in your Mac’s menu bar.

Common questions

How do I change my team?

Click the Puckbar icon in the menu bar and open Settings to pick a different team.

Scores or schedules aren’t updating

Puckbar needs an internet connection to fetch game data. Check that you’re online, then quit and reopen the app. If games still don’t appear, the schedule service may be temporarily unavailable.

How do I open Puckbar at login?

Go to System Settings › General › Login Items and add Puckbar to the list.

How do I remove Puckbar?

Quit the app from its menu, then drag Puckbar from your Applications folder to the Trash.
